My services

Piano Lessons and (Group) Theory Lessons

In Mega Piano studio, I am committed to provide lessons and guidance for everyone. Students who wish to take exams, need help with their music elective programme, or want to learn for leisure are all welcome! Each lesson will be tailored to meet the student’s needs.

Students will be building strong foundation on through solfeggi, rhythm training, and ear training. Piano lessons in MP Studio are also tied in with theory lessons, especially for students who are preparing for the graded exams. The goal is to deepen understanding and connection between music theory, music playing, and music listening. These are all important tools whether you aspire to be a professional musician or just want to enjoy music in your life.

For students preparing for their graded examinations, the fees start at SGD $60 for 45 minutes (Grade 1). Please contact me for more detailed pricing.

Mega Piano Studio also provides lessons for children between the age of 4-6. The fees is SGD $50 for 30 minutes. A 20-minute trial lesson also available only at SGD $30.

Group Theory Classes

Group theory classes are also available at MP Studio. Each class will have between 3-8 students (Grade 1-5) or 3-5 students (Grade 6-8). In group classes, students will not only be learning from the teacher, but also share ideas and experiences among their peers. In addition to learning music theory, group classes are a great way for students to be motivated to work hard, to encourage, and to learn from one another.

Collaborative Pianist (Accompaniment) service

In Mega Piano Studio, I provide accompaniment services for graded examinations, competitions, and other performance for all instruments and voice. Aural (ear) training are also included in coaching sessions. I also provide accompaniment for musical theatre, choir and ballet accompaniment.

Fees for accompaniment starts at $60, and it varies depending on grades (if you are preparing for graded exams), events and timeline. Please contact me for more detailed pricing.

Performances and Music Workshops

At Mega Piano Studio, music is about more than performance—it is about building connections, inspiring curiosity, and enriching our community through shared musical experiences. Through community performances, outreach events, workshops, and lecture-recitals, I strive to make classical music engaging and accessible to audiences of all backgrounds. Alongside the traditional classical repertoire, I am passionate about introducing listeners to contemporary classical music and uncovering the unique stories that every piece has to tell.

A memorable performance is built on more than technical excellence. While a solid technique and beautiful tone provide the foundation, it is the ability to communicate a compelling musical narrative that transforms a performance into a meaningful artistic experience. Every musician brings a unique voice, and every instrument possesses its own distinctive character and acoustic identity. Whether you are a performer, composer, educator, or part of an ensemble, I welcome opportunities to collaborate. Together, we can create meaningful musical experiences that inspire audiences, celebrate creativity, and tell stories that resonate.
