Games for learning

Learning music can be fun! We collaborate with a psychologist, game rule editor, game engine programmer, and passionate board game enthusiasts to design engaging games that make learning musical concepts an enjoyable and interactive experience.

Our board games combine thoughtful game design with music education, allowing players to explore and develop their understanding of musical concepts while having fun. By bringing together expertise from music, psychology, game development, and the board game community, we aim to create games that are both educational and genuinely enjoyable to play.

We put a great deal of heart, thought, and care into designing our board games, and our goal is to produce them to the highest possible quality. At the same time, we believe in being mindful of the environmental impact of production. Rather than printing large quantities that may go unused and eventually end up in landfill, we prefer to produce our games in quantities that reflect genuine interest and demand. This allows us to make more responsible use of resources and create high-quality games while minimizing unnecessary waste.

If you are interested in any of our games, please fill out the form below to let us know! Your response will help us gauge interest and plan our production accordingly. We will be in touch when the games are ready for production and available to order.

Thank you for supporting our games and helping us make music learning fun, thoughtful, and sustainable!
