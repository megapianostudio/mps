Mega Piano Studio

Courses and Services

Piano and Theory Lessons

Mega Piano Studio is committed to providing lessons and musical guidance for all. Students who wish to take exams, need help with their music elective programme, or want to learn for leisure are all welcome! Each lesson will be tailored to meet the student’s needs.

Students will be building strong foundation through solfeggi, rhythm training, and ear training. Piano lessons in MP Studio are also closely tied in with theory lessons, especially for students who are preparing for the graded exams. The goal is to deepen understanding and connect music theory, music playing, and music listening. These are all important tools whether you aspire to be a professional musician or just want to enjoy music in your life.

For students preparing for their graded examinations, the fees start at SGD $60 for 45 minutes (Grade 1).

Mega Piano Studio also provides lessons for young beginners between the age of 3.5-5. The fee is SGD $50 for 30 minutes.

A 20-minute trial lesson also available at only SGD $30.

Please contact us for more detailed pricing.

Group Theory Classes

Group theory classes are also available at MP Studio. Each class will have between 3-8 students (Grade 1-5) or 3-5 students (Grade 6-8). In group classes, students will not only be learning from the teacher, but also share musical ideas and experiences among their peers. In addition to learning music theory, group classes are a great way for students to be motivated to work hard, to encourage, and to learn from one another.

Contact us to join the waiting list.

Online Lessons

Interested to start a musical journey but not sure if you have the time to make the travel? Can’t find the right time that will work for you and the teacher? Do not let this be your barrier! MP Studio provides online lessons as well. Just make sure you already have the instrument (a piano or a keyboard), a good wifi, the Zoom meeting app (you can download it for free), and we can start anytime you like!

Please contact us for more detailed pricing.

Collaborative Pianist (Accompaniment) service

We provide accompaniment services for graded examinations, competitions, recitals, and a wide range of other performances. Accompaniment is available for instrumentalists and vocalists of all levels, with each collaboration tailored to the musical and performance needs of the individual. Coaching sessions also include aural (ear) training, helping students develop the listening and musicianship skills required for examinations and confident musical performance. In addition to instrumental and vocal accompaniment, we are also available for musical theatre productions, choir accompaniment, and ballet classes and performances.

Fees for accompaniment start at $60, and vary depending on the level and requirements of the examination, the nature of the event or performance, the amount of repertoire involved, rehearsal requirements, and the preparation timeline.

Please get in touch for more detailed pricing and to discuss your specific accompaniment needs.
